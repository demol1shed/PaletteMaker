## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Bottom navigation shell with a color harmony screen.
- Settings tab: appearance, palette defaults, sharing and export options, and an About section.
- Persistent app settings and a HEX output style (case, leading `#`).
- Adobe swatch export (`.ase` and `.aco`).
- Localization, export/share, accessibility, HEIC/AVIF support and CI.

### Changed
- Relicensed under Apache-2.0; third-party notices ship with the binaries.

### Fixed
- Empty dark toast shown above the navigation bar on the Settings tab.
- EXIF preview rotation, duplicate clipboard toast and easy-to-miss image load errors.
